The ship: a large Korean passenger car-ferry, about 146 metres long, with a long white hull and a tall white superstructure of several passenger decks lined with rows of small square windows, the navigation bridge near the bow, and a single funnel toward the stern. The underwater part of the hull is painted dark red.
